# Wild encounter counts

Regional forms and their evolutions are grouped with the main family of their species. Generated from `data/Encounters.c`. **Slots** counts encounter slots: a grass slot that holds the mon in the morning, day and night counts 1 (each time of day counts 1/3), with slot 1 merged into slot 0 and slot 8 merged into slot 2. Surf, rock smash and each rod slot count 1. **Areas** is the number of encounter tables the family appears in.

Skipped: tables with an encounter rate of 0, and grass/surf/rod tables that only contain Magikarp or Goldeen. Headbutt, swarms and the radio (Hoenn/Sinnoh sound) slots are not included.
